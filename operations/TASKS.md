# Tasks

If another tool owns tasks, put its link here and use that tracker instead.

| Task | Project or goal | Owner | Status | Due | Done when |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

Statuses: backlog, next, doing, blocked, done.

Write tasks as concrete actions. Keep one task in progress per person where practical. For blocked work, name the dependency and next follow-up. “Done” requires the stated result, not just time spent.
