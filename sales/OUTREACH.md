- Offer:
- Proof we may use:
- Channel:
- One small next step:

## Message

Write a short opener, explain the relevance, offer useful help, and ask one clear question. Never invent personal research or imply a relationship that does not exist.

## Follow-up

State what new value each follow-up adds and when to stop. Check applicable channel rules, consent requirements, and opt-outs before sending. A draft is not authorization to send.
